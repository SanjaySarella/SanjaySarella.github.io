About Sanjay

I am an AI Engineer with a Master’s in Data Analytics from Oklahoma City University. My focus is building intelligent systems that are production-ready and explainable — not just accurate.

Every predictive model I build is paired with SHAP attribution so stakeholders see exactly what drives each output. Every agentic pipeline uses Pydantic-typed state, structured reflection loops, and cited retrieval — so outputs trace back to their sources.

I am comfortable across the full stack: LangGraph workflows, RAG pipelines over real data, ML model training, and containerised FastAPI services on GCP Cloud Run. All four projects on this page are live and publicly accessible.
